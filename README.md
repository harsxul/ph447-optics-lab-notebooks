# PH447 Optics & Spectroscopy Lab – analysis notebooks

Nine Jupyter lab reports from the PH447 Physics Lab (Optics and Spectroscopy) at IIT Bombay, Autumn 2024.
Each notebook contains the aim, apparatus, raw data, analysis code (NumPy / SciPy / Matplotlib) and the write-up.

| # | Experiment | What is computed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Small-angle light scattering | Particle size of polystyrene beads from the scattering pattern |
| 2 | Mach-Zehnder interferometer | Refractive index of air vs. pressure from fringe shifts |
| 3 | Fourier optics / spatial filtering | FFT of lattice images compared with optical Fourier images; Köhler illumination |
| 4 | Quantum cryptography analogy | BB84 key exchange simulated with polarised light |
| 5 | Time-resolved absorption spectroscopy | Triplet-state decay time of ZnTPP from transient absorption traces |
| 6 | Hydrogen Balmer series | Balmer wavelengths and the Rydberg constant from CCD spectra |
| 7 | Zeeman effect | Fabry-Perot etalon spacing and the Bohr magneton from Zeeman splitting |
| 8 | Iodine absorption spectrum | Vibrational constant, anharmonicity and dissociation energy of I2 |
| 9 | Spin-orbit coupling of copper | Spin-orbit coupling constants of the 4F and 4D states |

Open any `.ipynb` in Jupyter or Google Colab. Data files were uploaded to Colab at run time; the plots and results are
saved in the notebook outputs.
